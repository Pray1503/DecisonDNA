## Discussion
Reviewing outage incident INC-0009 on security-scanner. Root Cause: A slow consumer thread due to synchronous downstream HTTP calls. As partition size grew, processing fell behind by over 50,000 messages, delaying order processing in NovaCommerce.. Lessons learned: Implement worker pool concurrency in consumer code, wrap calls in circuit breakers, and monitor consumer lag metrics to trigger auto-scaling.

## Decisions Made
Resolve post-mortem tasks. Approved mitigation and preventative actions: Implement worker pool concurrency in consumer code, wrap calls in circuit breakers, and monitor consumer lag metrics to trigger auto-scaling.
